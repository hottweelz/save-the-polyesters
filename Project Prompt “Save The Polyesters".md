# Project Prompt: “Saving The Polyesters”
## Satirical Environmental Activism / Mock Wildlife Conservation Campaign

Create a complete parody activism website for a fictional endangered species called “The Polyesters” or “Baby Polyesters.”

The project should intentionally blur the line between absurd satire and emotionally manipulative environmental advocacy in the style of modern activist campaigns, nonprofit wildlife organizations, social media outrage movements, and emotionally charged conservation groups.

The tone should feel:
- Dead serious on the surface
- Emotionally manipulative
- Visually heartbreaking
- Scientifically ridiculous if examined closely
- But believable enough that distracted or emotionally reactive people might initially accept it as real

The humor comes from:
- Treating polyester fabric as if it comes from living creatures
- Presenting “baby polyesters” as innocent fluffy animals harvested for sweaters and fast fashion
- Framing data centers, urban development, apartment complexes, and industrialization as habitat destruction events
- Mimicking real nonprofit environmental propaganda almost perfectly

This is SATIRE and PARODY.
The fictional species does not exist.

# Core Fictional Lore

## The Polyesters

Polyesters are fluffy creatures secretly farmed for synthetic clothing materials.

Claims made by the organization should include:
- Polyester fibers are harvested from living creatures
- Fast fashion drives mass polyester farming
- Industrial construction destroys polyester burrows
- Data centers create toxic runoff harming polyester lungs
- Apartment complexes destroy nesting grounds
- Gentrification pushes polyesters from protective habitats
- Artificial lighting disrupts breeding cycles
- Alligators prey on displaced polyesters in Florida and Georgia
- Feral hogs scavenge decomposing remains near construction zones
- “Microfiber shedding” is actually polyester fur loss caused by stress

The organization treats all of this as completely factual.

# Important

Do NOT break character.
Do NOT explain the joke on the website itself.
Do NOT acknowledge the parody internally.
Present everything as if the organization is completely real.

However:
- Avoid defaming real companies directly
- Avoid targeting real individuals
- Keep claims fictional and absurd enough to remain satire
- Use fictional organizations, labs, studies, and coalitions

The final result should feel like:
“Someone accidentally created the most emotionally manipulative fake environmental movement on the internet.”
