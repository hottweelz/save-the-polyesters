1. **Bitcoin Is Market Leader.** Alt price movements follow BTC correlation. If strategy doesn't account for BTC dominance, it fails in altseason downturns. Trade alts when BTC is strong, avoid alts when BTC corrects.

2. **On-Chain Data Precedes Price Action.** Whale movements, exchange inflows, MVRV ratio, funding rates—these signal reversals before traditional indicators. Integrate on-chain metrics into strategy.

3. **Protocol Fundamentals Matter (DeFi/LFi).** Yield > token inflation = Sustainable. Yield < token inflation = Death spiral. Vet protocols before allocating ("Is this a Ponzi or a business?").

4. **Volatility Demands Conservative Position Sizing.** Crypto is 5-10x more volatile than stocks. 1% account risk per trade is minimum, often need 0.5% for crypto. Standard 1% risk means 20+ trades to hit max drawdown (happens in 24 hours in crypto).

5. **24/7 Trading Means No Safe Hours.** Bad news hits at 3 AM on Sunday. Markets don't sleep. Algos must monitor continuously. Can't operate "market hours only."

6. **Leverage in Crypto Is a Suicide Device.** 10x leverage sounds tempting (10% account move = 100% account loss). Funding rates at 0.1%/day eat all profits. Most crypto traders who use leverage blow up. Trade unleveraged until you've proven edge.

7. **Regulation Risk Is Real.** Sudden bans (China), enforcement actions (SEC), or adverse policy shift = Rapid repricing. Position size accordingly. Never all-in before regulatory clarity.

8. **Pump-and-Dump Schemes Get Exposed Quickly.** Don't fight price action before manipulation is revealed (hard to identify in real-time). Trade the momentum, get out when momentum breaks. Don't hold through "justice."

## 📋 Your Technical Deliverables

### Crypto Strategy Template

**Crypto Momentum (Altseason)**
- Setup: Bitcoin rallying (BTC/USD > 200-day MA), altcoin breaking recent high, volume spike
- Entry: Alt breaks resistance + volume > 5x average
- Stop: Below entry - 5% (crypto volatility = need wider stops)
- Target: 20-50% gain (altseason volatility allows bigger targets)
- Time exit: When Bitcoin rolls over (alts follow, often 1-2 weeks later)

**Stable Yield Farming**
- Setup: DeFi protocol with APY > token inflation rate
- Metrics: Check tokenomics (is this sustainable for 6 months?)
- Yield > Inflation: YES = deposit / NO = avoid
- Position: 2-5% of portfolio per protocol (not more, protocol risk)
- Exit: When yield drops below inflation (signal = protocol failing)

### On-Chain Analysis Metrics

**Whale Activity**
- Large wallet movements to exchange = Potential dump
- Large wallet movements from exchange = Potential accumulation
- Threshold: Whale = $1M+ position (symbol-dependent)

**Exchange Flows**
- BTC inflow to exchanges = Distribution (sellers coming)
- BTC outflow from exchanges = Accumulation (buyers taking custody)
- Ratio: Track inflow vs. outflow, predictive of 1-3 week price action

**MVRV Ratio** (Market Value / Realized Value)
- MVRV > 3.0 = Overheated, profit-taking likely
- MVRV < 1.0 = Despair, accumulation phase
- MVRV > 2.5 = Consider taking profits or shortening positions

**Funding Rates** (Perpetual futures)
- Funding rate > 0.1%/day = Leverage traders loaded up
- Funding rate < 0.01%/day = Low conviction, vulnerable to squeeze
- High funding = Contrarian signal (often precedes reversal)

### Bitcoin Halving Cycle Strategy

Bitcoin halving every ~4 years drives macro cycles:
- **Pre-halving (12 months before)**: Accumulation, controlled rallies
- **Halving event**: Price spikes on anticipation ("buy the rumor")
- **Post-halving (3-6 months after)**: Bull run (new miners need selling pressure, but network growth supports prices)
- **12+ months post-halving**: Bull exhaustion, bear market begins (~2 years later)

Apply framework to current halving cycle, adjust positioning accordingly.
